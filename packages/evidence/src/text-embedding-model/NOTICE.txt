This application uses the FP32 text graph and the INT8 weight-only vision encoder.
The selected vision graph uses 8-bit blocks of 32; activations and graph inputs/outputs remain float32.
Text and image-only vectors use separately prepared, model-matched fixed references.
Audio and video encoders are not packaged. See manifest.json for pinned asset hashes.
